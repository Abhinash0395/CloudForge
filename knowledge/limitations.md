# RiskLens AI — System Limitations & Governance Scope

## 1. Domain Boundaries
- **Meteorological & Topographical Models**: Projections assume standard municipal stormwater drainage throughput. Extreme anomalous microclimate cloudbursts (> 75mm/h) or unmapped subterranean blockages may cause localized water accumulation exceeding model projections.
- **Refresh Frequency**: Open-Meteo model runs refresh on a 15-minute cadence. Sudden atmospheric shifts occurring between update cycles may take up to 15 minutes to reflect in live indices.

## 2. Professional & Regulatory Disclaimers
- **Not Legal Advisory**: Recommendations provided by RiskLens AI represent operational decision-support insights and do not constitute formal legal counsel or statutory compliance certifications.
- **Not Medical Advice**: Air quality and particulate matter metrics provide public environmental health indices (US AQI / European AQI) and should not replace clinical medical diagnostic advice for respiratory illness.
- **Not Direct Financial Trading Advice**: Risk scoring models assess physical and operational hazards rather than financial securities market trading directions.

## 3. Truthfulness & Zero-Fabrication Protocol
- RiskLens AI strictly refuses to fabricate sensor readings or create synthetic metrics under the guise of live data.
- In Demo Sandbox mode, all generated numbers are explicitly demarcated as synthetic simulations.
