# RiskLens AI — Technical Glossary

- **ASL (Above Sea Level)**: Elevation in meters referenced to global sea level datum. Crucial for assessing alluvial floodplain runoff vulnerability.
- **ECMWF (European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts)**: Leading global numerical weather prediction model ensemble integrated via Open-Meteo.
- **CAMS (Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service)**: European Union satellite and sensor atmospheric service providing air quality, aerosol, and greenhouse gas telemetry.
- **US AQI**: United States Air Quality Index standard (0–50 Good, 51–100 Moderate, 101–150 Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, 151–200 Unhealthy, 201+ Hazardous).
- **Relative Humidity (RH%)**: The ratio of current absolute vapor pressure to saturation vapor pressure at the observed temperature.
- **Barometric Pressure (hPa)**: Atmospheric pressure measured in hectopascals. Standard sea level pressure is approximately 1013.25 hPa; drops indicate approaching low-pressure storm systems.
- **Data Provenance**: Verifiable audit trail documenting data origin, API endpoint, retrieval timestamp, data quality score, and licensing.
- **Factor Perturbation**: Simulated percentage increase or decrease applied to an operational factor in what-if modeling.
- **Grounded AI**: Artificial intelligence whose responses are strictly bounded by verified database records, physical sensor telemetry, and documented knowledge base sources without extrapolation.
